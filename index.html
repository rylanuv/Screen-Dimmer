Screen Dimmer · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Screen Dimmer is designed to work on your device. The app does not operate its own servers, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or send your app settings to the developer.

Information the app handles

  • App settings: Your filter choices, presets, schedules, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. They are not uploaded by Screen Dimmer. They remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Accessibility service: If you enable it, Android allows Screen Dimmer to display its dimming overlay. The service does not retrieve screen text or other window content, and does not collect or transmit accessibility events.
  • Optional screen capture: If you enable the experimental midtone feature and approve Android's screen-capture prompt, the app temporarily mirrors display frames on your device to render the effect. Screen content is processed for that feature only; Screen Dimmer does not save or transmit those frames. You can stop the feature in the app or revoke screen-capture access through Android.
  • Optional device sensors: If you turn on ambient-light automation or shake gestures, the app uses light-sensor or accelerometer readings to perform that function locally. These readings are not stored as a history or sent to the developer.

Purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing for its optional premium unlock. Google processes purchase and payment information under its own privacy terms. Screen Dimmer receives purchase status so it can enable or restore the unlock; it does not receive your full payment card details. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's handling of data.

Information you choose to share

If you contact support, your email app sends your message and any details you include to rylpix.app@gmail.com. The email provider and mail app may process that information under their own policies. You choose whether to send a message.

Sharing and retention

The developer does not receive or share app usage data through Screen Dimmer. Local settings are retained on your device as described above. Purchase processing and any support email you send are handled by the relevant services and providers.

Children

Screen Dimmer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system or developer-operated data collection.

Your choices

You can disable optional accessibility, screen-capture, and sensor-based features in Android or in the app. You can remove locally stored app data through Android settings or uninstall the app. Purchase management is available through Google Play.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be shown on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact rylpix.app@gmail.com.
